GroundSurf by Akhilesh Khajuria

An offline, endlessly scrolling Chinese landscape wallpaper.
Supports Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 13 or later.
Intel compatibility is built into the binary but has not been tested on Intel hardware.

Drag GroundSurf.app onto Applications, then open it.
Use the GroundSurf menu in the menu bar to pause, change speed, create a new landscape, or quit.
Quitting reveals your previous wallpaper.

This preview is ad-hoc signed and NOT Apple notarized.
macOS may block the first launch of an app downloaded from the internet.
Read Apple's guidance before deciding whether to open it:
https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445

The app does not configure launch at login. Scenery stays at full detail.
Preparing ahead uses additional bounded memory. Overnight stability and battery use have not been established.
